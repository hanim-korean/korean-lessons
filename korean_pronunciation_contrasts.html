01Three Vowel Contrasts

Do not memorize these vowels as English spellings. Build a new mouth shape for each Korean vowel, then keep that shape steady from beginning to end.

One vowel = one steady target

03Why ㅁ Can Sound Like ㅂ—and ㄴ Like ㄷ

These pairs begin in nearly the same mouth place. The main difference is where the air goes and whether the sound continues or releases.

ㅁsame lip closureㅂ

Both sounds close the lips

That shared lip closure makes the pair look similar. The airflow creates the useful difference.

ㅁ · noseVoice and air continue through the nose: mmm.
ㅂ · mouthAir is held behind the lips, then released.
ㄴsame tongue areaㄷ

Both sounds use the tongue tip

The tongue tip closes near the gum ridge for both. Again, listen for airflow and continuity.

ㄴ · noseVoice and air continue through the nose: nnn.
ㄷ · mouthAir is held in the mouth, then released.
It is not always the learner's mistake. At the beginning of a word or phrase, some Korean speakers produce ㅁ or ㄴ with a weaker, shorter nasal cue. This is called partial initial denasalization. The result can sound a little like a voiced ㅂ or ㄷ to learners, even though the Korean speaker is using ㅁ or ㄴ. It varies by speaker and context, so do not imitate a full ㅂ or ㄷ. Keep a light nasal hum connected to the vowel.
1 · Feel the vibration

Touch the side of your nose lightly. Hold ㅁ or ㄴ before the vowel. You should feel nasal vibration; ㅂ and ㄷ cannot be held that way.

2 · Keep it connected

Practice mmm-아 → 마 and nnn-아 → 나. Shorten the hum gradually, but do not replace it with a strong release.

3 · Compare positions

Listen to initial 모두 / 나도, then medial 어머니 / 언니. The nasal cue may be easier to hear in the middle.

05The Five-Minute Routine

Use one contrast per day. Accuracy comes from short, repeated listening-and-speaking cycles.

1Watch

Make the two mouth shapes silently in a mirror.

2Listen

Tutor says ten mixed items. Student points left or right.

3Say

Student says five pairs slowly, then five in random order.

4Use

Choose two real words and put each into a short sentence.
