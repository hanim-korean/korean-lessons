Double Final Consonants

Two letters, one final position

What is a double final consonant?

A cluster such as ㄺ contains two different consonant letters at the bottom of a syllable: 닭. It differs from a doubled letter such as ㄲ or ㅆ.

Start with seven final sounds

At the end of a word, Korean final consonants reduce to seven sounds. Before another sound, further changes may occur.

The eleven clusters

ㄳ · ㄵ · ㄶ · ㄺ · ㄻ · ㄼ · ㄽ · ㄾ · ㄿ · ㅀ · ㅄ

Learn the usual final sound first. Then check exceptions and the next syllable. ㄶ and ㅀ need special attention because ㅎ can change the following consonant.

Usually keep the first consonant

These examples keep the first consonant as the final sound. The following consonant can also change, as in 많다 [만타] and 없다 [업따].

Watch the next consonant

In 없다, the final ㅂ sound makes ㄷ tense. In 앉다, tensification follows a separate rule for verb stems. In 많다 and 싫다, ㅎ combines with ㄷ to give ㅌ.

Usually keep the second consonant

Remember ㄺ → ㄱ, ㄻ → ㅁ and ㄿ → ㅂ at a word end. These are pronunciation patterns to learn, not a rule that one letter is “stronger”. Check the exceptions next.

밟- before a consonant

밟- usually gives a final ㅂ before a consonant. Before ㄴ, that sound changes to ㅁ. Before a vowel ending, ㄹ and ㅂ separate.

밟고[밥꼬]밟- → 밥 before ㄱ; ㄱ becomes ㄲ.
밟지[밥찌]밟- → 밥 before ㅈ; ㅈ becomes ㅉ.
밟는[밤ː는]Before ㄴ, the final ㅂ sound changes to ㅁ.
밟아[발바]Before the vowel ending, ㄹ stays and ㅂ links.
여덟 (compare)[여덜]Usually ㄼ → ㄹ.

The symbol ː marks vowel length in dictionary pronunciation.

Other ㄼ exceptions

밟- is not the only exception. Compare 넓다 [널따] with 넓죽하다 [넙쭈카다] and 넓적하다 [넙쩌카다]. Learn these words separately.

Before a vowel ending

Before a vowel-starting particle or ending, the second consonant usually starts the next syllable. Linked ㅅ becomes ㅆ. In ㄶ and ㅀ, ㅎ drops: 많아요 [마나요], 싫어요 [시러요].

A vowel-starting word works differently

Before a separate word or meaningful word part, first reduce the cluster to its final sound. Compare this with a vowel ending.

ExamplePronunciationWhat changes
값있어 (값 + 있다)[가비써]Before the word 있다, the reduced final ㅂ links.
없어요 (없 + 어요)[업써요]Before the ending -어요, ㅅ links as ㅆ.
